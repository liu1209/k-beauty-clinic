OUR STORY

Return to youth, naturally.

K-Beauty Clinic brings together the refined artistry of Korean aesthetic medicine and Canada's rigorous safety standards.

We don't take a one-size-fits-all, assembly-line approach. We look beyond your skin to your overall health, caring for you inside and out.

Drawing on many years of experience, we work to find the root cause rather than just treating symptoms. And because results continue at home, we offer regular home-care education.

Every laser technician on our team is trained personally by Dr. Yang. Whoever performs your treatment, it's tailored to you and held to Dr. Yang's standards.

To us, beauty isn't about looking different. It's about naturally returning to who you were. So we look at the balance of your whole face and body and combine exactly what you need.

As we age, the face is a lot like a tent. The poles weaken, the fabric sags, and heavy areas get heavier. We address all three together.

MEDICAL APPLIANCES

Genuine, professional-grade medical devices we use at K-Beauty Clinic. Every device treatment is performed by laser technicians trained personally by Dr. Yang, to her standards.

LIFTING & CONTOURING

Ultherapy

Focused ultrasound that lifts from the deepest layer

Delivers energy down to the SMAS layer to lift sagging facial contours.

Thermage

Radiofrequency tightening for firmer skin

Evenly heats the dermis to renew collagen and restore firmness.

ONDA PRO

Microwave contouring that slims and lifts

Reduces stubborn fat and firms loose skin, on both face and body.

LASERS

Fotona StarWalker

Q-switched laser that shatters pigment and ink

Treats melasma and pigmentation with toning, and colour tattoos with multiple wavelengths.

Soprano

Gentle, gradual-heating hair removal laser

In-Motion technology and cooling for comfortable hair removal, safer for darker skin tones.

REJUVENATION

Secret RF

Radiofrequency delivered through fine needles

For scars, pores, acne, and texture, and the treatment our own team gets most.

PRP Kit

Dedicated kit for platelet-rich plasma

Skin renewal, hair loss, and joints, using your own growth factors.

OUR PROMISE

Root causes, not just symptoms.

The same concern can have different causes in different people. We look at your overall health, not just your skin, to find the cause, and recommend only what you need.

Personal care, not an assembly line.

We never apply the same treatment to everyone. We care for you inside and out, combining treatments to suit your face and body.

We never compromise on safety.

Strict sterilization and disinfection, genuine devices and products only, and only fillers that can be dissolved.

Regular home-care education.

Great results are completed by what you do every day. Our regular home- care education helps your results last.
