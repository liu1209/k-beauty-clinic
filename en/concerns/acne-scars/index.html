Why it happens

01

Oil, bacteria and inflammation

Clogged pores become inflamed breakouts.

02

Hormones and stress

Adult acne often follows hormonal cycles, stress and diet.

03

Collagen damage

Deep, inflamed breakouts damage collagen and leave pitted or rolling scars.

How we approach it

We clear active acne first, then treat what it left behind. Red or brown marks and true scars are different problems with different treatments. For deep, tethered scars, Dr. Yang can release the scar under the skin and fill it. For acne that keeps coming back, she also looks at hormones, gut health and diet.

Inside-and-out care

DR. YANG'S NOTE · ON ACNE

“
Acne is a skin condition with many causes hiding behind it. The best known is teenage hormones, and stress is known to be the biggest cause of adult acne. Many women also get breakouts with every period because of hormonal changes. Irregular sleep, an unhealthy diet, or foods that simply don't suit your body can all make acne a sign that something isn't right on the inside. That's why I take a thorough history first, and try to treat the root cause alongside the skin.”

Dr. Eun Young Yang, ND · Director, K-Beauty Clinic

DR. YANG'S NOTE · ON SCARS

“
Scars form for many reasons, and they take many shapes: raised above the skin, sunken and pitted, or changed in colour. Because there are so many possibilities, the first step is to analyse the type of scar and its cause. With sunken scars, heavily fibrous tissue under the skin is often holding the scar down, so releasing that tissue is the key to treatment.”

Dr. Eun Young Yang, ND · Director, K-Beauty Clinic
