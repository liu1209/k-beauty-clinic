Why it happens

01

Overactive sweat glands

Some people’s glands produce far more sweat than the body needs.

02

Genetics

It often runs in families.

03

Triggers

Stress, heat and caffeine make it worse.

How we approach it

We confirm the area and severity, then treat with small injections that block sweat signals in that area.
