Why it happens

01

Sun

UV exposure adds up over the years and shows as sun spots, age spots and freckles.

02

Hormones

Melasma is linked to hormones, pregnancy and birth control, and gets worse with sun and heat.

03

Inflammation

Breakouts, rashes and irritation can leave brown marks behind.

How we approach it

Not every brown spot is the same. Sun spots and melasma need almost opposite approaches — too much heat can clear a sun spot but trigger rebound pigmentation in melasma. We identify your melasma type and depth first, then set every laser precisely for your skin. Low-heat toning is especially suited to Asian skin and medium to darker skin tones. All treatments follow strict sterilization and safety protocols, and Dr. Yang can also look at hormonal and internal factors.

DR. YANG'S NOTE · ON PIGMENTATION

“
Treating pigmentation is never simple. Before we begin, I need to decide which of several types of pigment to treat first, and which sits deeper or closer to the surface. And pigment keeps being made — clearing it once doesn't mean it will never come back. That's why it's one of the hardest areas to treat. I've heard that fewer clinics take on pigmentation these days, as so many treatments have become quick, device-driven ones. Pigmentation is more like driving a manual car. It isn't solved in one or two sessions; it takes real rapport and trust between doctor and patient. When we start today, I need to be able to see where your skin will be in six months and in a year. That's why experience matters so much here.”

Dr. Eun Young Yang, ND · Director, K-Beauty Clinic
