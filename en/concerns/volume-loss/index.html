Sound familiar?

My face looks tired and gaunt.

I have hollows under my eyes and around my temples.

My cheeks have flattened.

I lost weight and now my face looks older.

Why it happens

01

Fat pads shrink and shift

Cheeks flatten and folds deepen.

02

Bone changes

The face loses some of its structure with age.

03

Weight loss and exercise

Losing body fat often shows first in the face.

How we approach it

We restore support in small, careful amounts and keep your face looking like you. We use only dissolvable hyaluronic acid fillers that last two years or less, and Sculptra when you prefer a slow, gradual change.

All injectable treatments — neuromodulators, fillers, Sculptra and SKINVIVE — are performed personally by Dr. Eun Young Yang, ND.

DR. YANG'S NOTE · ON VOLUME

“
You rarely see a young, bright face that looks hollow or gaunt. That's how important volume is to looking healthy and youthful. But when volume builds up where it shouldn't be, the face looks heavy and older instead. Through years of liposuction and fat-grafting surgery, I studied closely where to add volume and where to take it away, and I believe that experience helps me create good results.”

Dr. Eun Young Yang, ND · Director, K-Beauty Clinic
