What is Laser Genesis? Laser Genesis is a gentle 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser treatment that warms the skin to reduce facial redness, rosacea flushing and small broken capillaries, while stimulating collagen to refine pores and fine lines. There is no downtime.

Genesis uses a long-pulse 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser to gently warm the skin. It calms small visible vessels and redness and encourages new collagen, so skin looks smoother and more even.

It's gentle enough for sensitive, redness-prone skin, and you can go straight back to your day.

Genesis: Your Questions, Answered

  • Is Laser Genesis good for rosacea and sensitive skin?

Yes. Genesis is one of the gentlest lasers we offer and is often used for redness-prone skin.

  • When will I see results from Laser Genesis?

Many people notice a clearer look after the first session. Redness and pores improve over the series.

  • Does Laser Genesis hurt?

It feels warm. No numbing is needed.

Can I wear makeup after Laser Genesis?

Yes, usually the same day.

How many Laser Genesis sessions do I need?

Usually 4‒6, about 4 weeks apart.
