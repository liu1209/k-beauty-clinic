What is medical weight management? Medical weight management is a doctor-supervised weight-loss program. At K- Beauty Clinic in Coquitlam, naturopathic doctor Dr. Yang combines lab testing, nutrition, regular follow-ups and, where appropriate and safe, prescription treatment.

Weight isn't decided by willpower alone. Hormones, metabolism, sleep and stress all play a part.

Dr. Yang starts with your health history and lab work, then builds a personal plan that can include nutrition, lifestyle support and, where appropriate and safe, prescription treatment.

Weight Care: Your Questions, Answered

Can a naturopathic doctor prescribe weight-loss medication in BC?

Where it is appropriate and safe for you, Dr. Yang can prescribe within her scope as a naturopathic doctor.

Do I need blood work before starting a weight-loss program?

Usually, yes. It helps us find hormonal or metabolic causes.

Can I combine medical weight loss with body contouring?

Yes. Weight care changes the whole body; ONDA Pro refines specific areas.

How often are weight-loss follow-up visits?

Usually every four weeks to start.

Is naturopathic weight loss covered by insurance?

Naturopathic visits are often covered by extended health plans. Please check your plan.
