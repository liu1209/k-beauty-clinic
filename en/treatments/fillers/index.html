Contouring that sculpts your face.

HA fillers are made from hyaluronic acid, a substance your skin produces naturally. They restore volume and refine contours instantly, so you see results right away. For your safety, K-Beauty Clinic uses only HA fillers that can be dissolved immediately if needed.

We choose products of different firmness for each area and depth. Areas that need structure, like the forehead and chin, and areas that need softness, like the lips and cheeks, each get the product that suits them best.

Video: how HA fillers work

An HA filler works like sculpting: the shape forms the moment it's placed. That's why where, how deep, and how much decide the result.

Hyaluronic acid draws in moisture, so treated areas look naturally plump and supple. It's gradually absorbed by the body over time, and if needed, it can be dissolved with a dedicated enzyme.

Fillers: Your Questions, Answered

  • Who are fillers for?

Fillers are a great fit if hollow cheeks or temples or a flat forehead make you look tired or older, or if you're bothered by deep smile lines or a short chin. You'll see the difference right after treatment.

  • How are contouring fillers different from regular fillers?

Regular fillers focus on filling hollows. Contouring fillers design the shape of your whole face. We look at the flow from forehead to cheeks to chin, and at your profile, then slim heavier areas with ONDA PRO and fill hollow areas with filler for a smaller, more sculpted face.

  • Will lip filler give me duck lips?

Not when it's done right. Poorly placed lip filler pushes the lips forward and creates that duck-lip look. Beautiful lips don't jut out. The upper lip should gently flip upward and lift along with the corners of the mouth. We work with your lip borders and upper-to-lower ratio, adding small amounts with precision for lips that look natural and full of life.

  • Will more filler make me look better?

No. Too much filler can make the face look puffy and expressions look unnatural. Beauty comes from balance, not volume. We look at the proportions of your whole face and place only what's needed, only where it's needed.

  • How long does filler last?

It depends on the product, the area, and your metabolism, but results usually last six months to a year or more. Areas that move less tend to last longer.

  • Can I get filler in an area where I've had cosmetic surgery?

It depends on when you had surgery. If it was recent, it's best to wait until the area has fully healed and settled before we assess whether filler is right. Some people want filler right away because they're unhappy with their surgical result, but we generally recommend waiting until the skin and tissue have stabilized.

  • I've had a fat transfer to my face. Can I still get filler?

Yes. But if the fat transfer has left tissue under the skin stuck together, it's important to release it before treatment. Placing filler directly into that tissue can make it less effective, so please let us know about any past fat transfer at your consultation.

  • Is there a longer-lasting filler?

Some fillers last five years or more. But facial trends change, and over time filler can shift with the movement of your muscles. Semi- permanent and permanent fillers that last five years or longer can also cause problems later if your immune system weakens. That's why we believe fillers lasting two years or less are the safest choice, and that's what we use.

  • Can fillers help with deep, pitted scars?

Yes. Deep scars are often tethered by fibrous bands beneath the skin that pull it down, so filling alone may not lift them. By first releasing those bands with needle subcision and then filling the space with filler, we can lift the depressed area, making this combination very effective for deep scars.

  • Can you treat delicate areas like the under-eyes or nose?

Yes. The tear trough has thin skin and many blood vessels, so it demands a very skilled hand, and the nose also requires a deep understanding of its blood supply to be shaped safely. With more than 25 years of clinical experience, we treat these challenging areas naturally and safely. We can also create fresh, lifted apple cheeks or give the ears a fuller, softer shape.

  • Can filler be reversed if I don't like it?

Yes. HA fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase, so adjustments are possible. Still, the best approach is never overdoing it in the first place, by designing with the balance of your whole face in mind.

  • Does it hurt or bruise?

We use numbing cream, fillers that contain anesthetic, and a blunt cannula when appropriate to reduce pain and bruising. Depending on the area, you may have some mild swelling or bruising for a few days, which usually settles on its own.

  • Is filler safe? What about blocked blood vessels?

Filler is a treatment where experience matters most, because some problems can be hard to reverse. Drawing on more than 25 years of clinical experience, we choose placement, depth, and amount with your facial blood vessels in mind to minimize the risk of complications like vascular occlusion. We use only HA fillers that can be dissolved immediately, and we're prepared to respond right away if anything comes up.

  • Can I get filler if I have a nose or smile-line implant?

In principle, yes. But depending on which layer the implant sits in and how it's positioned, it can be more difficult, so we need to see you in person to check the implant's placement. Filler over an implant is more complex and calls for a different technique, so experience matters even more.

  • I'm unhappy with filler I had done elsewhere. Can it be redone?

If it's HA filler, we can dissolve it with hyaluronidase and redo it. That said, because it's hard to know exactly where and how much was injected elsewhere, it may not be possible to remove it 100% cleanly. We generally recommend waiting until the existing filler has partly absorbed and your skin has settled. If you need it redone sooner, for example because you live overseas, we'll assess the area carefully in person first.

  • Can I combine filler with other treatments?

Yes. Lift sagging areas with Ultherapy + Thermage and fill hollows with filler, and each enhances the other. We can also slim heavier areas with ONDA PRO, rebuild broader hollows with Sculptra, and refine the jawline with Liftox, on the same day or spaced apart depending on your skin.

Aftercare

The first week after filler affects both its shape and how long it lasts.

The first week

Don't press or rub the treated area, and try not to sleep face-down.

  • For swelling, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time over the first day or two.
  • Skip saunas, intense workouts, and alcohol for three to four days.
  • Avoid facials, facial massage, and RF treatments for about two weeks.
  • You can wear light makeup from the next day.
  • Call us right away if the area turns white or purplish, or if pain keeps getting worse.

Individual results may vary.
