Platinum Peel

Platinum Peel is a gentle peel that takes our LalaPeel one step further. It combines the antioxidant benefits of platinum with PHA for exfoliation and LHA for pore and oil care, in an optimal balance.

Rather than stripping the skin, it's designed to minimize irritation and restore your skin's natural, healthy condition, so even sensitive skin can enjoy it comfortably.

A gentle peel for antioxidants, exfoliation, and pores in one step

Dead skin · Breakouts · Pores · Oil-moisture balance · Tone

Video: inside a Platinum Peel

When dead skin and oil build up, skin looks dull, pores clog and break out, and makeup won't sit well. Free radicals from stress and UV also leave skin tired and aging faster.

Platinum Peel uses PHA to gently smooth away dead skin, LHA to manage oil inside pores, and platinum to help protect against free radicals. Together, they bring skin back to a clear, healthy state.

Platinum Peel: Your Questions, Answered

How is it different from LalaPeel?

LalaPeel focuses on dead skin and oil. Platinum Peel adds the antioxidant benefits of platinum along with PHA and LHA, reducing irritation and helping restore skin condition. If you've had LalaPeel, it's the natural next step.

Who is it for?

It's a great fit if your makeup looks flaky, you break out often, your oil- moisture balance is off, your skin looks dull, or you want a simple peel that also improves texture and firmness.

Will my skin peel? Is there downtime?

It's not an aggressive peel, so most people have no visible peeling and go straight back to their day. You may have some temporary redness that settles quickly.

Does it hurt?

You may feel brief tingling or itching, but most people find it comfortable.

How often should I have it?

We usually recommend five to ten sessions, spaced one to two weeks apart. The more consistently you come, the steadier the improvement in texture and tone.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. It's a gentle peel, so sensitive skin usually tolerates it comfortably. We adjust application time and coverage to your skin.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. A facial after your peel helps the care work deeper, and alternating with Pico Toning or Secret RF helps you manage both tone and texture.

Home Care

A few days of moisturizing and sun protection protect your results.

Moisturize and protect

After a peel, your skin is rebuilding its outer layer. Apply moisturizer and sunscreen diligently.

  • Avoid saunas, hot baths, and intense workouts on the day of your peel.
  • Pause scrubs, exfoliants, and retinol for two to three days.
  • If any flaking appears, don't pick at it; let it shed naturally.
  • Call us right away if redness or stinging persists.
