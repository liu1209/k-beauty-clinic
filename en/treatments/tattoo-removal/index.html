Fotona StarWalker Tattoo Removal

StarWalker is a Q-switched laser from Fotona, a laser company based in Slovenia. It delivers powerful energy in extremely short pulses, making it well suited to breaking down tattoo ink beneath the skin.

Different ink colours respond to different wavelengths. StarWalker offers multiple wavelengths (including 1064 nm and 532 nm), so we can treat not only black ink but also colours like red and orange.

The laser breaks up the ink, and your body clears it away

Black · Red · Orange · Cosmetic tattoos · Eyeliner

Video: how StarWalker tattoo removal works

When the laser reaches the ink particles in your skin, it shatters them into tiny fragments. Your immune system then gradually absorbs those fragments and clears them from the body. That's why a tattoo doesn't disappear in one session, but fades a little more each time.

The right wavelength and energy depend on the ink's colour, depth, and density. Precise energy lets us break up the ink effectively while minimizing damage to the surrounding skin and the risk of scarring.

Tattoo Removal at K-Beauty Clinic

Tattoo removal results depend more on the practitioner than the device. Even two black tattoos can need very different settings depending on whether they were done professionally or by an amateur, how deep the ink sits, and your skin tone. Tattoo Removal: Your Questions, Answered

How many sessions will I need?

It varies widely with the tattoo's colours, size, depth, ink density, and location. Amateur and cosmetic tattoos often fade in fewer sessions, while dense, colourful professional tattoos may need ten or more. We'll look at your tattoo at your consultation and give you an honest estimate.

How far apart are sessions?

Your body needs time to clear the broken-down ink and your skin needs time to heal, so sessions are usually spaced six to eight weeks apart. Giving your skin enough time between sessions helps reduce scarring and improves results.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as a rubber band snapping against the skin. We apply numbing cream beforehand and use cooling during treatment to keep you comfortable.

Will it leave a scar?

Treating with the right energy and allowing enough time between sessions helps lower the risk of scarring. Aftercare matters too, especially leaving scabs alone. Any scarring from the original tattoo may remain.

Will my tattoo be completely gone?

Most tattoos fade significantly, and many can be removed to the point where they're barely visible. Some inks and colours, especially green, light blue, and white, may not clear completely. We'll be upfront with you about what to expect.

Can you remove cosmetic eyebrow or eyeliner tattoos?

Yes. Note that cosmetic tattoos containing skin-tone or white pigments can darken when treated with a laser, so we always do a small test spot first.

What's recovery like?

The area may be red and swollen, and small blisters or scabs can form over the next few days. As long as you keep the area protected, most people go straight back to their day.

Aftercare

How you care for the area after treatment makes a big difference to your results and to preventing scars.

Leave scabs alone

Blisters and scabs are part of healing. Picking or scratching them can cause scarring, so let them fall off on their own.

  • Apply the ointment we recommend and keep the area clean.
  • Avoid saunas, swimming, and hot baths for a few days.
  • Keep the area out of strong sun, and once healed, apply sunscreen diligently.
  • For swelling, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time.
  • Staying well hydrated and getting enough rest helps your body clear the ink.
  • Call us right away if you notice signs of infection, such as severe pain, oozing, or heat.
