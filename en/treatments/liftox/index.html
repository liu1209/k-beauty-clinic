Liftox is a signature Botox face lift designed exclusively at K- Beauty Clinic. It builds on the well-known Nefertiti lift (a Botox lift for the jawline and neck) and brings together the benefits of classic wrinkle relaxers and skin Botox (microtox).

The Nefertiti lift focuses mainly on the lower face. Liftox adds Dr. Yang's own mid-face lift, skin-texture technique and left‒right balancing, gently lifting the whole face.

FACIAL BALANCE

With age, the two sides of your face drift apart.

The two sides of the face don't age at the same speed, and the difference often becomes clearly visible after 50. In many patients, Dr. Yang also sees signs of small, earlier nerve or muscle changes that the person never noticed.

  • One eyebrow, cheek or corner of the mouth sits lower
  • Your smile pulls more to one side
  • One side of your face looks older in photos

Lasers, fillers and lifting devices treat both sides in much the same way, so they can't correct this kind of imbalance on their own. With Liftox, Dr. Yang assesses and doses each side separately — relaxing the side that pulls harder and lifting the side that has dropped — to bring your face back into balance. It's what we are proudest of.

“
As we age, the face doesn't just sag. It loses its balance. Some muscles start pulling the face down, while other areas lose their strength. That's why I came to believe that restoring the balance of the whole face matters more than erasing a single wrinkle. Liftox was born from that idea. It brings together the best of classic wrinkle relaxers and microtox, then adds a natural lift. A face where nobody can tell what you had done, but everyone notices you look younger. That's what Liftox creates.”

Liftox: Your Questions, Answered

Liftox vs. Nefertiti lift: what's the difference?

A Nefertiti lift is a Botox lift that focuses on the jawline and neck — the lower face. Liftox includes that lower-face lift and adds Dr. Yang's own mid-face lift, skin-texture technique and left‒right balancing, so the whole face looks lifted and refreshed.

Is Liftox the same as skin Botox or microtox?

Skin Botox focuses on texture and fine lines. Liftox adds muscle balancing and a natural lift, so it targets all three at once, with placement, depth, dose and distribution designed for every face.

Can Botox fix an uneven or asymmetrical face?

Often, yes. Because each side is assessed and dosed separately, Liftox can correct imbalance that has built up over the years — something most other non-surgical treatments can't address.

Who is a good candidate for a Botox face lift?

It's especially good if your jawline is softening, the corners of your mouth are turning down and your face is starting to look out of balance, and if you want to look younger without surgery or downtime.

Does Botox hurt?

We apply numbing cream first. It feels about the same as a regular wrinkle-relaxer injection: a few quick pinches.

How long does Botox last?

You'll usually notice a change in three to seven days, with full results at around two weeks. Your best results last three to four months, and for some people the effect continues for up to six months.

Can I combine Botox with Ultherapy, Thermage or fillers?

Yes. Ultherapy + Thermage lifts the deeper layers while Liftox refines balance and texture. It also pairs well with filler, Sculptra and SKINVIVE.

Aftercare

The first 4 hours

Stay upright, and don't rub or press the treated area. This gives the product time to settle exactly where it was placed.

  • Skip saunas, intense workouts and alcohol on the day of treatment.
  • It's best to wait until the next day to wear makeup.
  • Avoid facial massage and RF treatments for two weeks.
  • Any small marks or swelling usually fade within a few hours.
  • Call us right away if your eyelids feel heavy or your expressions feel uncomfortable.
