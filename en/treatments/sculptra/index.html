Sculptra®

Sculptra is a collagen-stimulating injectable made from PLLA (poly-L-lactic acid). Since its FDA approval, it has been used for years to restore facial volume and soften wrinkles.

Unlike regular fillers that add volume instantly, Sculptra stimulates your body's own collagen, so volume builds gradually from your own skin. The PLLA is naturally absorbed over time, leaving your own collagen in its place.

PLLA stimulates collagen, so volume builds from your own skin

Hollow cheeks · Temples · Smile lines · Jawline · Firmness

Video: how Sculptra stimulates collagen

Sculptra's results don't appear overnight. They start to show at around one month and develop fully over three to six months, then last around two years.

Because the volume comes from your own collagen, it looks and feels natural, and it improves more than just volume. Firmness and texture get better too. It's the ideal choice if you want to naturally look like yourself five or ten years ago.

Sculptra at K-Beauty Clinic

With Sculptra, what matters isn't the volume right after treatment. It's predictive design: planning for the collagen that will build over the coming months. Even with the same amount, how much collagen forms depends on your skin thickness, skin type, and age. With more than 25 years of clinical experience, Dr. Yang pictures your face at three and six months and plans the amount and depth for every area.

We also review your treatment history carefully. If you've had filler, fat grafting, or thread lifts, your preparation and technique need to change depending on the condition of your tissue.

Unlike HA fillers, Sculptra can't be dissolved, so experience matters even more. We carefully manage dilution, injection depth, and aftercare to minimize the risk of side effects such as nodules.

Sculptra: Your Questions, Answered

  • I've heard Sculptra doesn't work. Is that true?

Sculptra's results appear gradually, starting at around one month, so people used to the instant results of filler sometimes assume it isn't working. Results also depend heavily on dilution, injection depth, and aftercare. When it's done correctly, you'll see satisfying volume build over three to six months.

  • What's the biggest advantage of Sculptra?

How natural it looks. Because the volume comes from your own collagen, it has a soft, natural feel that's hard to achieve with regular filler, and your skin's firmness and texture improve too. That's why so many people come back for it two or three years later.

  • How many sessions will I need?

Usually two to three sessions, four to six weeks apart, using about one vial per area. We adjust the number of sessions and amount based on how much volume you've lost.

  • I've heard it has a lot of side effects. Is it safe?

You may have some bruising or swelling for a few days. Occasionally, small bumps (nodules) can form under the skin, but these are usually related to technique and aftercare rather than the product itself. Precise dilution and depth, plus following the post-treatment massage, greatly reduce the risk.

  • What face shapes and ages is it best for?

It's especially well suited to people in their late 30s and beyond, when volume loss starts to make the face look thin or tired. Results still vary with face shape and skin type at any age, so we'll tell you honestly whether Sculptra is right for you or whether another treatment would serve you better.

  • Is there any downtime?

Very little. You'll notice some fullness right after treatment that fades within a few days. That's completely normal. Mild bruising or swelling may last a few days, but it won't get in the way of your routine.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. Lifting sagging areas with Ultherapy + Thermage, or adding HA filler to the smile lines or chin for an instant boost, can give even better results. If your skin is thin and dry, we also recommend skin boosters.

Aftercare

With Sculptra, aftercare shapes your results. Above all, follow the massage routine.

The 5-5-5 rule

Massage the treated area gently for 5 minutes, 5 times a day, for 5 days. It's the most important step for smooth, even results and for preventing nodules.

  • The fullness you notice right after treatment will fade within a few days. That's normal. The real results start building at around one month.
  • For swelling, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time over the first day or two.
  • Skip saunas, intense workouts, and alcohol for three to four days.
  • Stay well hydrated, and use moisturizer and sunscreen to give your skin the best environment for building collagen.
  • Call us right away if you feel a firm lump, or if swelling or pain doesn't settle.
