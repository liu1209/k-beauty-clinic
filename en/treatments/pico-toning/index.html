Pico Toning

Pico Toning uses ultra-short laser pulses, measured in picoseconds (trillionths of a second), to break up pigment in the skin.

Traditional laser toning relies mostly on heat. A picosecond laser shatters pigment before heat has time to spread, so the surrounding skin stays calm. That makes it a gentler, safer choice for Asian skin, which tends to be prone to pigmentation.

Ultra-short pulses break up pigment and brighten your tone

Melasma · Sun spots · Dull skin · Pores

Video: how picosecond lasers break up pigment

A picosecond laser delivers energy in an extremely short burst, breaking pigment into tiny particles through a photoacoustic effect rather than heat. Your body's own immune cells then clear those particles away naturally.

Less heat means a lower risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, the darkening that can follow aggressive treatment. The gentle stimulation also refines texture, pores, and overall tone.

Pico Toning: Your Questions, Answered

Who is Pico Toning for?

It's a great fit if you're dealing with melasma, sun spots, freckles, or marks left behind by acne, or if your skin tone looks dull or uneven. It's also ideal if you want to improve pores and texture with steady care and no downtime.

Does it hurt?

It feels like a light snap of a rubber band. Most people are comfortable without anesthesia, and we can apply numbing cream if your skin is sensitive.

How many sessions will I need?

Many people notice a brighter tone after one or two sessions. For clear, lasting results, we recommend 10 or more sessions, one to two weeks apart. Even more important than the number, though, is an accurate diagnosis and the right energy for your skin and pigment. Energy that's too weak or too strong makes treatment much harder.

Is there any downtime?

No. You might have slight redness for a few hours, but you can go straight back to work. Makeup can go on the same day or the next.

Can melasma come back?

Yes. Melasma is affected by sun exposure, hormones, and skin irritation, so it can return. That's why daily sun protection and regular maintenance are important even after it fades.

How is Pico Toning different from regular laser toning?

Regular toning uses nanosecond pulses and relies on heat. Pico Toning uses picosecond pulses, a thousand times shorter, to break pigment into much finer particles. There's less heat on the surrounding skin, so it's more comfortable with a lower risk of pigmentation. On top of that, we stimulate collagen alongside toning, so we're not just breaking up pigment. We're making your skin healthier, and that's why results are better.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. The best partner for Pico Toning is skin boosters. Pigment tends to form more easily in dry skin, so treating dehydration with skin boosters means less pigment comes back and toning results last longer. PRP can support renewal, and Ultherapy + Thermage can add firmness, on the same day or one to two weeks apart.

Aftercare

Home care matters as much as the treatment itself. Dry skin is especially prone to pigment coming back, so protect it with sunscreen and moisturizer every single day.

Sunscreen, every day

This is the most important step. Throughout your treatment, wear sunscreen even on cloudy days and indoors, and reapply every two to three hours.

  • Moisturize right after cleansing to prevent hidden dryness. Well-hydrated skin is your best defence against pigment coming back.
  • Skip saunas, hot baths, and heavy drinking for two to three days.
  • Pause scrubs, peels, retinol, and other harsh products for three to five days.
  • Don't rub or scratch the treated area.
  • Stay well hydrated to support your skin's recovery.
  • Call us right away if you notice strong redness, blistering, or darkening.
