Microneedling meets radiofrequency.
Secret RF

Secret RF is a skin-renewal treatment that uses ultra-fine microneedles to deliver radiofrequency (RF) energy to exactly the depth your skin needs.

Together, the micro-stimulation from the needles and the heat from RF prompt your skin to rebuild its own collagen and elastin. One of its biggest advantages is that it treats not only acne scars and pores, but also skin that's still actively breaking out.

Microneedles deliver RF energy to rebuild collagen

Acne · Acne scars · Pores · Firmness

Video: how RF microneedling works

Needle depth can be adjusted in fine increments, so energy reaches exactly where it's needed, from shallow concerns like scars and pores to deeper ones like loss of firmness.

As the treated skin heals, it produces new collagen and elastin. Depressed scars fill in, enlarged pores tighten, and texture and firmness improve together.

RF energy also acts on the sebaceous glands that produce oil, reducing oil production. That's why skin looks less shiny and breakouts calm down. Because it doesn't resurface the top layer of skin, the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) stays low even in summer, and it's gentle enough to repeat regularly to keep collagen building.

Matching depth and energy to your skin.
Secret RF at K-Beauty Clinic

Even with the same device, results depend heavily on the depth and energy used. We first assess the shape and depth of your scars, the condition of your pores, and your skin's thickness and sensitivity, then set a different needle depth and energy for each area.

Secret RF isn't about injuring your skin. It's about helping your skin heal and rebuild itself. That's why we finish every treatment with our K-Beauty Clinic Signature Serum to support recovery. Most patients are back to their normal routine the next day with little to no heat or discomfort.

Secret RF: Your Questions, Answered

  • Who is Secret RF for?

It's especially good for acne scars, enlarged pores, and rough texture. It also helps oily skin that's still breaking out, fine lines around the eyes and mouth, and early loss of firmness. It's safe for most people, whatever your skin type and whatever the season.

  • Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream thoroughly beforehand, so most people feel only a prickly warmth. We adjust the energy by area and depth, and check in with you throughout.

  • Is there any downtime?

Very little. We apply our Signature Serum right after treatment to speed up recovery, so most people are back to normal the next day with little to no heat or discomfort. Slight redness or tiny marks may last a day or two, and most can wear makeup the next day.

  • Can I get it in summer, or if my skin is sensitive?

Yes. Secret RF delivers energy below the surface without resurfacing it, so the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is low even in midsummer. We adjust the depth and energy to your skin, so sensitive skin can be treated safely, and it's gentle enough to repeat regularly.

  • Can I get it while I still have active acne?

Yes, and it's actually a great choice. RF energy acts on the oil-producing sebaceous glands and reduces oil production, so your skin looks less shiny and breakouts calm down. At the same time, new collagen helps prevent future scarring.

  • How many sessions will I need?

Many people notice smoother texture and smaller-looking pores after one session. We usually recommend three to five sessions, four weeks apart, and deeper acne scars may need more. Because collagen takes time to build, your skin keeps improving for two to three months after your last session.

  • Will my acne scars disappear completely?

For deep, long-standing scars, the realistic goal is to make them noticeably shallower and smoother rather than erase them completely. Depending on the type of scar, combining treatments can improve results, and we'll be honest with you about what to expect.

  • Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. The tiny channels Secret RF creates help skin boosters and PRP absorb more deeply for better results. If pigment is also a concern, it pairs well with Pico Toning, on the same day or spaced apart depending on your skin.

Aftercare

Secret RF works as your skin heals itself, so how you care for it in the first few days really shapes your results.

The first 24 hours

Skip makeup and cleanse gently with lukewarm water only. This is when the tiny channels in your skin close, so try not to touch the treated area.

  • Apply the Signature Serum and repair cream we send home with you, as directed.
  • Wear sunscreen every time you go outside.
  • Skip saunas, intense workouts, and alcohol for two to three days.
  • Pause scrubs, peels, retinol, and other harsh products for one week.
  • If you notice scabbing or flaking, don't pick. Let it fall off naturally.
  • Call us right away if you notice severe swelling, blistering, lasting heat, or signs of infection.

Individual results may vary.
