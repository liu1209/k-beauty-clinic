What is a food sensitivity test? A food sensitivity test is a blood test that helps identify foods that may be linked to ongoing symptoms like bloating, digestive discomfort, skin flare-ups, headaches and fatigue. It is not an allergy test. Results are used to guide a short elimination and reintroduction plan.

Food Sensitivity Testing: Your Questions, Answered

Is a food sensitivity test the same as an allergy test?

No. Allergy testing looks for immediate reactions such as hives or trouble breathing. A food sensitivity test helps find foods that may be linked to slower, ongoing symptoms like bloating, skin flare-ups and fatigue. True food allergies need assessment by your doctor or an allergist.

How is the test done?

With a simple blood draw. When your results are ready, you review them one-on-one with Dr. Yang.

Will I have to avoid those foods forever?

No. Foods are usually reduced for 4‒6 weeks, then reintroduced one at a time. Many can be eaten again.

Can food sensitivities affect my skin?

Gut health affects the skin. Many people find that caring for the inside alongside skin treatments helps.

Is food sensitivity testing covered by insurance?

Naturopathic visits are often covered by extended health plans. The lab test is usually billed separately; please check your plan.
