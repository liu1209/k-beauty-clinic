PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) is a skin-renewal treatment that concentrates the platelets in your own blood, which are packed with growth factors that support healing, and returns them to your skin.

Because it uses your own body's components rather than anything foreign, the risk of reactions is very low, and it wakes up your skin's natural ability to repair itself and build collagen. We use a dedicated kit designed to produce platelet-rich plasma.

Beyond the skin, PRP is widely used to treat hair loss, and because it supports tissue repair, it's also used in joint regeneration.

Growth factors from your own blood wake up skin renewal and collagen

Texture · Fine lines · Under-eyes · Scars · Hair loss · Joints

Video: blood draw, centrifuge, and treatment

We draw a small amount of blood and spin it in a centrifuge to separate the platelet-rich layer, which is full of the growth factors your skin needs to repair itself. When this concentrated PRP is placed into the skin, it supports healing and stimulates new collagen and blood vessels.

That's why PRP improves more than one concern. It lifts your overall skin health: texture, fine lines, dull tone, thin under-eyes, and scars all get better, and it helps you recover faster from other treatments, too.

PRP: Your Questions, Answered

  • What is PRP?

We draw a small amount of your blood, spin it in a centrifuge, and collect a concentrated layer of platelets rich in growth factors. That PRP is then placed back into your skin, waking up its natural ability to heal and build collagen.

  • Who is PRP for?

It's a great fit if your texture has become rough and fine lines are increasing, if your skin looks thin or dull, if your under-eyes look hollow or dark, or if you're bothered by acne scars. It's also ideal if you want to recover faster from other treatments.

  • Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream thoroughly beforehand, so most people feel only a mild sting. The blood draw feels just like a routine blood test.

  • Is there any downtime?

Very little. You may have tiny marks or mild swelling for one to three days, and some bruising is possible under the eyes. You can return to your day right away and wear makeup from the next day.

  • Can PRP help with hair loss or joints?

Yes. PRP delivers growth factors around the hair follicles, helping hair grow back thicker and healthier, which is why it's widely used for hair loss. Because it supports tissue repair, it's also used in joint regeneration. The number of sessions and approach depend on your condition, and we'll walk you through it at your consultation.

  • Is it safe since it's my own blood?

Yes. Because it uses your own body's components, the risk of allergies or reactions is very low. We prepare it hygienically using a dedicated kit designed to produce platelet-rich plasma.

  • How many sessions will I need?

We usually recommend three sessions, four weeks apart. Results build gradually over one to three months as new collagen forms, and one or two maintenance sessions a year keep your skin at its best.

  • Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. With Secret RF, the tiny channels it creates let PRP absorb deeply for better results on scars and pores. With Ultherapy and Thermage, you boost both firmness and the health of the skin itself.

Aftercare

PRP works through your body's own renewal, so how you care for your skin in the first few days affects your results.

The first 24 hours

Skip makeup and cleanse gently with lukewarm water only. Don't rub or touch the treated area.

  • Skip saunas, intense workouts, and alcohol for two to three days.
  • Avoid anti-inflammatory pain relievers (like ibuprofen) for a few days before and after treatment, as they can weaken platelet activity. Let us know about any medications you take.
  • Stay well hydrated, and keep up with moisturizer and sunscreen.
  • For bruising under the eyes, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time.
  • Call us right away if you notice severe swelling, heat, or pain that doesn't settle.

Individual results may vary.
