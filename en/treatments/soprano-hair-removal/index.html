Soprano Laser Hair Removal

Soprano is a diode laser hair removal system from Alma Lasers in Israel, and one of the most widely used hair removal platforms in clinics around the world.

Traditional hair removal lasers fire one strong pulse at a time. Soprano glides continuously over the skin, gradually heating the follicles (In-Motion™), while a built-in cooling system keeps the surface comfortable. Most people say it feels like a warm massage.

Gently heats the follicle to stop regrowth, comfortably

Underarms · Legs · Bikini · Face · Back · Chest

Video: how Soprano In-Motion hair removal works

Laser energy follows the pigment in the hair down to the follicle, weakening the part that makes hair grow back. Soprano builds that energy up gradually with repeated passes, heating the follicle thoroughly without the sharp sting of traditional lasers.

Because the energy isn't delivered in one intense burst, it's gentler on the skin, which makes it a safer option for darker or tanned skin tones. Hair grows in cycles, so treatment is done over several sessions, with hair becoming finer and sparser each time.

CAUSE FIRST

Unwanted hair should be treated by its cause.

If hair is new or suddenly increasing, it may not be just a hair problem. In women, coarse hair growing on the chin, jawline, upper lip, chest or tummy can be a sign of hormonal change.

  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) — coarse hair together with irregular periods, acne or weight changes.
  • Menopause — as estrogen drops, male-type hormones have more effect, and coarse hair can appear on the chin and upper lip.
  • Other hormone imbalances — checked with lab work where needed.

If the cause is left alone, new hair can keep growing back after laser. That's why we check for hormonal causes before laser. As a naturopathic doctor, Dr. Yang reviews your symptoms and lab work, supports the hormonal side with natural supplements, nutrition and lifestyle care, and refers you to your family doctor when needed.

Soprano Hair Removal: Your Questions, Answered

Does it hurt?

Soprano gradually heats the follicles and cools the skin at the same time, so most people feel only a gentle warmth, like a warm massage. In most cases, no numbing cream is needed. Men's hair is denser and coarser, so the first few sessions can feel stronger. It becomes much more comfortable as the hair thins, and we adjust the settings to your comfort.

Is it permanent?

After a full course, hair is noticeably reduced and finer, and you can enjoy smooth skin for a long time. It's semi-permanent: most people only need a maintenance session every few years. If new hair grows because of hormonal change, we look after the cause, too.

How many sessions will I need?

Hair grows in cycles, so it can't all be removed at once. We usually recommend six to eight sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart for the face and six to eight weeks apart for the body. The exact number depends on your hair thickness, the area, and hormonal factors.

Can I get it if I have darker or tanned skin?

Yes. Because Soprano builds energy gradually, it's a safer option for darker skin tones. Freshly tanned skin is more easily irritated, though, so avoid strong sun exposure before and after treatment.

Does it work on all hair colours?

Lasers target the pigment in hair, so they work best on dark, coarse hair. Results are limited on white, grey, or very light blonde hair, and we'll let you know at your consultation.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. If areas like your underarms or bikini line have darkened, adding Pico Toning helps even out your skin tone, too.

How should I prepare?

Shave the area the day before your appointment. Because the laser needs the follicle in place, avoid waxing, plucking, or epilating for four weeks beforehand. Avoid tanning and strong sun exposure before and after treatment.

Could my facial hair be caused by PCOS?

It can be. New or increasing coarse hair on the chin, jaw, chest or tummy, especially with irregular periods, acne or weight changes, can be a sign of PCOS. We check for this at your consultation, and Dr. Yang can support the hormonal side alongside laser.

Why am I getting chin hair after menopause?

As estrogen falls, male-type hormones have relatively more effect, so coarse, darker hair can appear on the chin, jaw and upper lip. Laser works well while the hair is still dark, and the hormonal side can be supported with natural supplements, nutrition and lifestyle care.

Is laser hair removal good for men?

Yes. For men, it's a great-value treatment with high satisfaction. Back, chest, shoulders, beard line and neck are the most popular areas. Denser, coarser hair may need a few extra sessions.

Laser hair removal vs waxing: which is better?

Waxing lasts a few weeks, so it has to be repeated for life, and the constant pulling can cause dark patches, bumps and ingrown hairs. Laser hair removal is semi-permanent: after a full course, most people only need maintenance every few years.

Before & After Care

How you prepare and care for your skin makes a big difference to both results and safety.

Sun protection

Avoid strong sun and tanning before and after treatment, and apply sunscreen to any exposed treated areas.

  • Shave the area the day before. Avoid waxing or plucking for four weeks beforehand.
  • Skip hot showers, saunas, and intense workouts for 24 to 48 hours afterward.
  • If the area feels warm or looks red, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time.
  • Hair will start to shed naturally one to two weeks after treatment. Gentle exfoliation can help.
  • Call us right away if you notice blistering, strong redness, or stinging that doesn't go away.
