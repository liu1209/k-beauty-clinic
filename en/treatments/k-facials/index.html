K-Facials that give your skin what it needs

Our facials aren't just about relaxing. Each one is professional care designed around your skin. We'll recommend the right one of three programs based on what your skin needs most right now.

The Circulation Facial is for puffy, dull skin; the Hydration Facial for dry, tired skin; and Acne Care for breakouts and excess oil. Paired with treatments, they support recovery and help results last.

Three professional programs tailored to your skin

Circulation · Hydration · Acne Care

Video: inside a K-Facial

Your skin reflects your body's circulation and hydration. When circulation is sluggish, your face looks puffy and dull; when hydration is low, skin turns rough; and when oil and dead skin build up, breakouts follow.

K-Facials combine hands-on massage, professional products, and skincare devices to release what's blocked, replenish what's missing, and calm what's irritated. It's our inside-out philosophy: caring for body and skin together.

K-Facials: Your Questions, Answered

I'm not sure which facial to choose.

We'll check your skin first and recommend the best fit. Choose Circulation for puffiness and dullness, Hydration for dryness, and Acne Care for breakouts.

How often should I come in?

We usually recommend every two to four weeks. Regular care in step with your skin's renewal cycle keeps it in steady, healthy condition.

What does the Circulation Facial do?

Lymphatic massage releases sluggish circulation in the face and neck. Many people notice less puffiness and a brighter complexion right away, and tension in the neck and shoulders eases too.

Will extractions leave scars?

We extract hygienically with sterilized tools, only as far as your skin can safely handle. We never force inflamed acne; instead we calm it and may recommend treatments like Secret RF or Platinum Peel.

Can I have a facial on the same day as a laser treatment?

It depends on the treatment. The Hydration Facial pairs with many treatments, but right after some lasers your skin needs to avoid stimulation, so we'll advise you on order and timing.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. We adjust products and massage pressure to your skin, focusing on calming care for sensitive or red skin.

Can I wear makeup afterward?

Yes, right away after Circulation and Hydration facials. After Acne Care, keep to light basic skincare that day so extraction areas can settle.

Home Care

Your skin looks its best when clinic care and daily home care work together.

Stay hydrated

Circulation and hydration start from within. Aim for 1.5 to 2 litres of water a day.

  • Avoid saunas and hot baths on the day of your facial.
  • After Acne Care, avoid touching extraction areas.
  • Moisturize and apply sunscreen diligently every day.
  • Our regular home-care education sessions will show you how to care for your skin type.
