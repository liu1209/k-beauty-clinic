Can filler be dissolved? Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. It is used to correct filler migration, lumps, puffy under-eye filler or an overfilled look, usually with visible results within 24‒72 hours.

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. It's used when filler has migrated, looks puffy or uneven, or no longer suits your face.

We can also dissolve filler done at another clinic, as long as it's hyaluronic acid. We check where it is and how much is there first, then dissolve only what's needed.

Filler Dissolving: Your Questions, Answered

  • Can you dissolve filler done at another clinic?

Yes, if it's a hyaluronic acid filler. Bring any details you have about the product used.

  • Is dissolving filler safe?

We check for allergies first and monitor you after treatment. Reactions are uncommon.

  • How fast does hyaluronidase work?

Usually within 24‒72 hours.

Does hyaluronidase dissolve your natural tissue?

It mainly breaks down hyaluronic acid filler. Your natural tissue recovers its normal levels quickly.

How long after dissolving filler can I get filler again?

At least two weeks later, once your face has settled and we've reassessed.
