Ultherapy + Thermage

We combine Ultherapy and Thermage in a single treatment plan. Because each works at a different depth, together they firm your skin layer by layer, from the deep foundation all the way to the surface.

Ultherapy isn't a laser. It uses focused ultrasound and is FDA- cleared and Health Canada-licensed for non-surgical lifting. Thermage uses radiofrequency (RF) energy and has been one of the most trusted names in skin tightening since its FDA clearance in 2002.

Ultherapy lifts the SMAS. Thermage tightens the dermis.

Jawline · Jowls · Double chin · Fine lines

Video: how Ultherapy and Thermage work

Ultherapy delivers ultrasound energy to the SMAS layer, the same foundational layer a surgeon tightens during a facelift, to lift sagging contours. Thermage heats the dermis with radiofrequency, tightening collagen for firmer, smoother skin.

Neither treatment adds anything to your skin. Both help it rebuild its own collagen, so results look natural and keep improving over two to three months.

A lift that knows where to pull and what to protect.
Ultherapy + Thermage at K-Beauty Clinic

We never treat two faces the same way. We first assess your facial fat, bone structure, skin thickness, and how your muscles move, then customize the depth and number of shots for every area. We look closely at facial fat and volume, protecting areas prone to hollowing, like the cheeks, and focusing where you need lift.

When it comes to combining treatments, more isn't always better. We only recommend combinations that are proven safe together and truly complement each other, done on the same day or one to two weeks apart depending on your skin.

Ultherapy + Thermage: Your Questions, Answered

  • Is it too much to do Ultherapy and Thermage on the same day?

Not at all. Thermage targets the dermis, while Ultherapy reaches the deeper SMAS layer, so they work at different depths. We tailor the energy to your skin, so combining them is safe. Any redness, swelling, or tenderness usually settles within a few days.

  • When will I see results, and will I sag once they fade?

Results build gradually over two to three months as new collagen forms, and usually peak at three to six months. When they eventually fade, they fade gradually. There's no rebound that leaves you looking worse than before. Most patients maintain their results with a yearly treatment.

  • Will Ultherapy make my face look hollow?

It can if a slim face is over-treated at deeper levels. That's why we assess your facial volume and structure first, and adjust the depth and shot count in areas prone to hollowing.

  • Should I wait until I'm older?

There's no right age. In your 30s, it works preventively by building collagen early. From your 40s on, it improves sagging that's already there. If your skin laxity is very advanced, we'll be honest about what non-surgical lifting can and can't do.

  • Does it hurt?

You'll feel heat or brief zaps of energy, especially over bony areas. We apply numbing cream, offer pain relief if needed, and adjust the intensity as we go. Most patients find it very manageable.

  • Is there any downtime?

No. You can go straight back to work, and makeup can go on the same day or the next. Some redness, mild swelling, or tenderness may last a few days.

  • Do I really need 600 shots of Ultherapy?

No. More shots don't mean better results. The right number depends on your face size, how much sagging you have, your skin thickness, and how much fat you carry. What matters is treating the right areas at the right depth, with only as many shots as they need. Going beyond that just adds discomfort and swelling, and on a slim face it can even leave the cheeks looking hollow. We plan your shots around your face, not a number.

  • Will I still need other treatments?

Ultherapy + Thermage is the foundation for firmness and lift. Adding Sculptra or filler for lost volume, Liftox for the jawline, and SKINVIVE for radiance can take your results further, and Secret RF in between helps them last longer.

It's been two weeks since my Thermage and I don't see a difference yet.

That's completely normal. The real results appear as new collagen forms over two to three months. Compare with your before photos and give it a little more time. If you don't see a change after three months, come in for a follow-up.

Aftercare

  • Moisturize generously. Skin can feel dry after treatment.
  • Wear sunscreen every time you go outside.
  • Stay well hydrated to support your skin's recovery.
  • For warmth or swelling, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time.
  • Skip saunas, heavy drinking, and harsh exfoliation for about a week.
  • Don't rub or massage the treated area hard.
  • Call us right away if you notice worsening pain, blistering, or numbness that doesn't go away.
