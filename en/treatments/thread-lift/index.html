Lift sagging tissue back where it belongs.

A thread lift uses dissolvable medical threads placed beneath the skin to physically lift sagging tissue, no surgery required. Tiny barbs along each thread grip the tissue and lift it upward, so you can see a difference right away.

Over time, the threads are naturally absorbed by the body, and as they dissolve they stimulate collagen production, improving firmness too.

Video: how a thread lift works

Ultherapy + Thermage uses energy to stimulate collagen and lift gradually. A thread lift physically lifts sagging tissue right away, so the lift is noticeably stronger than Ultherapy or other lasers, and it's especially effective once sagging has become noticeable.

You'll see a lifted line immediately after treatment, and over the following months, collagen builds around the threads so the lift settles in and holds.

Thread Lift: Your Questions, Answered

  • Who is a thread lift for?

It's a great fit if your jawline has softened and your jowls and smile lines have visibly sagged, or if energy-based lifting like Ultherapy + Thermage alone hasn't given you enough change. It's a strong option if you want a real lift without surgery.

  • Does it hurt?

We use local anesthetic, so most people are comfortable during treatment. You may feel some tightness or tenderness for a few days afterward.

  • Will I be able to feel or see the threads?

Because we place threads at the right depth for your tissue and skin thickness, they're usually not visible. You may notice some tightness or slight puckering early on, but it typically smooths out within a few weeks.

  • How is it different from Ultherapy and Thermage?

Ultherapy and Thermage use energy to stimulate collagen and firm the skin gradually over a few months. A thread lift physically grips and lifts sagging tissue, so it delivers a noticeably stronger lift than Ultherapy or other laser treatments, and you see a difference right away. Together, they give you both an instant lift and longer-lasting firmness.

  • What about swelling, bruising, and recovery?

Bruising can last two weeks or more, and swelling can take up to a month to fully settle. Any tight feeling usually eases within one to two weeks. Most people return to daily life the next day, but if you have an important event, we recommend leaving about a month before it.

  • How long do results last?

It depends on the type of thread and your skin, but results typically last one to two years. The collagen formed as the threads dissolve keeps supporting the skin, and maintenance with Ultherapy + Thermage or Secret RF helps results last even longer.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. If your face has lost volume, restoring the foundation with Sculptra before your thread lift gives a much more natural result. Liftox to rebalance the face and Ultherapy + Thermage to maintain firmness also pair beautifully.

Aftercare

How you care for your skin in the first few weeks, while the threads settle, shapes your results.

The first 2 weeks

Avoid opening your mouth wide, chewing hard foods, facial massage, and exaggerated expressions. This is when the threads settle into place.

  • For the first week, sleep on your back and avoid sleeping face-down or on your side.
  • You can cleanse and wear makeup from the next day. Be gentle and avoid rubbing the treated area.
  • Skip saunas, intense workouts, and alcohol for one to two weeks.
  • Schedule dental work or anything that requires keeping your mouth open for long periods at least two weeks out.
  • Call us right away if swelling or pain gets worse, or if you notice heat or redness that could signal infection.

Individual results may vary.
