K-Beauty IV Therapy

IV therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants directly into your bloodstream. Oral supplements lose much of their potency during digestion and absorption, while IV nutrients go straight to work.

We've organized our IV therapy into three programs: V for Vitamin (energy), D for Detox, and C for Anti-aging. Choose based on what your body and skin need right now, and we'll fine-tune the formula at your consultation. We also offer an IV Weight Support Program for people working on weight loss.

Over time, stress, poor sleep, and irregular meals deplete your body's nutrients and increase free radicals. That shows up not just as fatigue, but in your skin too: dullness and loss of firmness.

IV therapy works by replenishing what's missing and fighting free radicals, helping your body recover, so your skin can be healthier too.

IV Therapy: Your Questions, Answered

  • How is this different from taking supplements?

Oral supplements are limited by how much your gut can absorb. IV therapy delivers nutrients directly into your bloodstream at higher levels, and faster. It works well alongside your daily supplements.

  • How long does it take?

About 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the program. Just sit back and relax.

  • Is it safe for everyone?

Most people can have IV therapy, but if you have kidney or heart conditions, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a G6PD deficiency, your formula may need adjusting or IV therapy may not be suitable. That's why we always review your health and medications first.

  • Are there side effects?

You may notice slight bruising or soreness at the injection site, a warm feeling, or a vitamin taste in your mouth. These usually fade quickly.

  • Which program is right for me: V, D, or C?

If you're tired and running low, try V. If you feel heavy or puffy, or you're recovering from a stretch of late nights, try D. If your concern is skin tone and firmness, try C. We'll help you choose and adjust the formula at your consultation.

  • How often should I come in?

It depends on your body, how you feel, and your goals, so it's different for everyone. We'll set a schedule that's right for you at your consultation.

  • Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. Pairing IV therapy with laser or lifting treatments supports recovery and can enhance results. You can even do both on the same day.

  • Can IV therapy help with weight loss?

IV therapy doesn't burn fat on its own. But weight loss can leave you tired and run down, so our IV Weight Support Program helps support energy and metabolism alongside Dr. Yang's medical weight management plan.

Before & After Care

A little preparation makes your IV therapy more comfortable and more effective.

Stay hydrated

Drink plenty of water before and after your session. It makes your veins easier to find and supports circulation.

  • If you have recent blood test results, please bring them. They help us tailor your formula more precisely.
  • Don't come in on an empty stomach. Have a light meal beforehand.
  • Tell us about any medications, supplements, and health conditions at your consultation.
  • Press firmly on the injection site for a few minutes afterward to minimize bruising.
  • Avoid alcohol on the day of your treatment.
  • Let us know right away if you feel dizzy or your heart is racing, or if the injection site becomes very swollen or painful.

Individual results may vary.
