The same brown spot can need a very different treatment.

What is the best treatment for melasma and pigmentation? There is no single best laser for every pigment. Melasma, sun spots, freckles and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation need different approaches. The K-Clear program diagnoses the pigment first, then combines Laser Genesis, nano toning, pico toning and pico fractional as needed, with relapse care.

Melasma, sun spots (solar lentigines), freckles and post- inflammatory hyperpigmentation can look alike, but their causes, depth and response to treatment are all different. Repeating the same laser at the same setting may help some pigment, while sensitive pigment like melasma can become darker or blotchy.

K-Clear starts by identifying exactly what kind of pigment you have, then considers your skin type, pigment depth and risk of inflammation to choose and combine Laser Genesis, nano laser toning, pico toning and pico fractional treatments as needed.

K-Clear Pigmentation: Your Questions, Answered

Why is pico toning more expensive at some clinics?

K-Clear isn't a single laser session. It includes pigment diagnosis, a tailored combination of four devices, progress checks at every visit and relapse care. Pigment treated the wrong way can get darker, so doing it right from the start saves time and money.

Can melasma be cured permanently?

Melasma is a chronic pigment affected by hormones, sun and heat, so the goal is to fade it and keep it faded rather than a permanent cure. Sun spots and freckles often improve faster.

Can laser toning make melasma worse?

Depending on your skin, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation or a melasma flare can happen. That's why we assess inflammation risk first and adjust devices and settings at every visit.

How many laser sessions do I need for melasma?

It depends on the type and depth of your pigment. After your diagnosis at the first visit, we'll explain the expected number of sessions and timeline.

Can naturopathic care help melasma?

Yes. As a naturopathic doctor, Dr. Yang looks at hormones, sleep and stress, and may recommend natural supplements and lifestyle support where helpful.

Aftercare

Sunscreen

Half of pigment treatment is what you do afterward.

  • Apply sunscreen daily and reapply every 2‒3 hours outdoors.
  • Avoid saunas, hot yoga and strong heat, which can trigger melasma.
  • Use your recommended home care products consistently.
  • Contact us right away if an area becomes unusually red or darker.
