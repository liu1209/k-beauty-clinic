TMJ BOTOX · GRINDING · CLENCHING · HEADACHES

We treat the temples,
not just the jaw.

Grinding and clenching strain the temporalis muscle at the temples as well as the masseter in the jaw. That's why many people wake up with a tired, tight jaw or keep getting headaches at the temples.

  • Your jaw feels tight or tired in the morning
  • Someone has told you that you grind or clench your teeth
  • You often get headaches at the temples
  • Your jaw muscles have grown and your face looks square

Our TMJ Botox assesses and treats both the masseter and the temporalis, reducing excess muscle force and helping to ease tension in the jaw and temples. Where needed, we refer you to a dentist for a night guard, and recommend acupuncture and massage alongside.

Botox: Your Questions, Answered

  • How long does Botox last?

Results are usually at their best for 3‒4 months, and for some people last up to 6 months.

  • Can Botox help teeth grinding and TMJ pain?

Yes. TMJ Botox reduces excess force in the masseter and temporalis muscles, which can help ease grinding, clenching, jaw pain and temple headaches. Where needed, we refer you to a dentist for a night guard.

  • Does Botox hurt?

A very fine needle is used, so most people feel only a brief pinch.

  • Will Botox make me look frozen?

No fixed dose is used. We dose by your muscle strength and expressions, so keeping your expressions natural is the priority.

  • How many jaw slimming Botox sessions do I need?

The jaw muscle takes time to shrink, so a few sessions usually give a more stable, lasting result.

  • Is there downtime after Botox?

No. Just avoid saunas, intense exercise and rubbing the treated area on the day.
