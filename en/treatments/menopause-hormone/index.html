How can a naturopathic doctor help with menopause? A naturopathic doctor can assess symptoms such as hot flashes, poor sleep, mood changes and weight gain, order lab tests, and build a plan focused mainly on natural supplements, nutrition and lifestyle changes, with hormone therapy considered only where truly needed.

Around perimenopause and menopause, sleep, mood, energy, weight and skin all change together. Hot flashes, sleepless nights and sudden weight gain are natural, but you don't have to just put up with them.

As a naturopathic doctor, Dr. Yang reviews your symptoms and lab work, then supports your hormone balance mainly with natural supplements, nutrition and lifestyle changes. Hormone therapy is considered only where it's truly needed and safe.

Hormone Care: Your Questions, Answered

  • What natural supplements help with menopause symptoms?

As a naturopathic doctor, Dr. Yang mainly recommends natural supplements, nutrition and lifestyle support. Hormone therapy is considered only after assessment and lab work, where it's truly needed and safe.

  • Can hormone therapy help skin and hair during menopause?

Hormones affect both. Many patients combine this care with skin treatments.

  • Are naturopathic visits covered by insurance?

Naturopathic visits are often covered by extended health plans.

What age does perimenopause start?

No. Perimenopause often starts in the 40s, and early support helps.

Do I still need my family doctor?

Yes. We work alongside your family doctor.
