SKINVIVE Skin Booster

SKINVIVE is a hyaluronic acid skin booster from the JUVÉDERM® family, best known for fillers. Unlike filler, which adds volume, SKINVIVE is placed in tiny microdroplets evenly through the skin to improve hydration and texture.

It doesn't change the shape of your face. Instead, it leaves skin smoother, deeply hydrated, and naturally radiant. It also contains a local anesthetic (lidocaine) for a more comfortable treatment.

Hyaluronic acid placed evenly in the skin, hydrating from within

Dehydration · Texture · Glow · Fine lines

Video: how SKINVIVE microdroplets work

Hyaluronic acid occurs naturally in your skin and holds many times its weight in water, keeping skin supple and hydrated. With age and dry climates, your skin's hyaluronic acid declines, leaving it rough and dull.

SKINVIVE replenishes hyaluronic acid in tiny, evenly spaced microdroplets in the dermis. That way it addresses deep dehydration that moisturizers can't reach, restoring smoothness and radiance from within.

We start by fixing dehydrated skin.
SKINVIVE at K-Beauty Clinic

Many skin concerns, from melasma and pigmentation to fine lines, develop more easily and last longer in dehydrated skin. That's why we treat replenishing hydration as just as important as lasers or lifting. Once deep dehydration is resolved, other treatments last longer too.

SKINVIVE looks its most natural when it's spread evenly. Drawing on more than 25 years of clinical experience, Dr. Yang carefully adjusts the depth, amount, and spacing of each injection to your skin's thickness and condition.

SKINVIVE: Your Questions, Answered

  • How is SKINVIVE different from filler?

Filler adds volume to shape hollow areas. SKINVIVE places hyaluronic acid in tiny microdroplets evenly through the skin to improve texture, hydration, and glow. Your face keeps its shape; your skin simply gets better.

  • When will I see results, and how long do they last?

Once any swelling settles after a few days, you'll notice smoother, more hydrated skin. Results vary, but typically last around six months, and regular treatments help maintain them.

  • Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream beforehand, and the product itself contains an anesthetic, so most people feel only a slight pinch.

  • Who is SKINVIVE for?

It's a great fit if your skin feels dry or dull, your makeup doesn't sit well, your texture feels rough, or you've noticed fine lines on your cheeks. We also recommend it alongside melasma or pigmentation treatment.

  • How many treatments will I need?

Most people see a difference after one treatment. Depending on your skin, a touch-up about a month later may be recommended. We'll advise you at your consultation.

  • Can I go back to my day right after?

Yes. You may have small bumps, redness, mild swelling, or bruising at injection points for a few days, but most people carry on as usual. If you have an important event, we recommend leaving one to two weeks before it.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. With Pico Toning, resolving deep dehydration helps keep pigmentation from coming back and makes toning results last longer. Pair it with Liftox to rebalance the face, or with Ultherapy + Thermage for firmness, and you get both glow and lift.

Aftercare

A few days of care after treatment help create an even, natural result.

Stay hydrated

Hyaluronic acid works by drawing in water. Drink plenty of water after treatment and moisturize diligently.

  • Don't rub or press the treated area on the day of treatment, and wait until the next day to apply makeup.
  • Avoid saunas, hot baths, intense workouts, and alcohol for two to three days.
  • Small bumps usually settle on their own within a few days, so please don't press on them.
  • Apply sunscreen diligently every day.
  • Call us right away if you have severe pain, lasting swelling, or any change in skin colour.
