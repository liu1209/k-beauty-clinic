ONDA PRO is a microwave-based body contouring device developed by DEKA in Italy.

Where earlier devices relied on radiofrequency or ultrasound, ONDA uses microwave energy to do two things at once: reduce stubborn fat and tighten the skin over it.

The latest PRO model delivers more power for faster treatments, and its precision handpieces let us work on everything from the jawline to the abdomen.

Microwave energy targets fat and firms the skin

Double chin · Bra fat · Upper arms · Banana roll

Video: how ONDA PRO works

ONDA PRO's patented Coolwaves® technology sends microwave energy straight to the fat layer. Very little is absorbed at the surface, and a built-in cooling system keeps your skin comfortable, so the treatment is powerful where it counts and gentle everywhere else.

Treated fat cells break down and are naturally cleared by your body over the following weeks. At the same time, the connective tissue beneath the skin contracts. The result isn't just less fat. It's a slimmer, firmer, more defined shape.

ONDA PRO: Your Questions, Answered

  • Who is ONDA PRO for?

It's ideal for stubborn fat that won't budge with diet and exercise, and for loose skin after weight loss. It's especially effective on areas that bother even slim people as they get older, like bra fat, upper arms, and the banana roll, which are notoriously hard to treat. On the face, it slims a double chin and jowls for a sharper jawline.

  • How many sessions will I need, and how long do results last?

Many people see a difference after just one session. Results start to show at around four weeks and keep improving over two to three months. Depending on the area, two to three sessions spaced four weeks apart usually give the best results. As long as your weight stays stable, the results last.

  • Will my skin sag once the fat is gone?

ONDA PRO tightens the skin as it reduces fat, so it helps prevent the loose look that can follow fat loss. We only treat areas with excess fat, and where volume is lacking, we restore balance with filler or Sculptra.

  • Does it hurt?

Very little. Unlike treatments that heat the skin's surface, ONDA PRO delivers energy beneath the skin while a cooling system keeps the surface comfortable. Most people say it feels like a warm compress, and no anesthesia is needed. It's a great option if pain has kept you away from other treatments.

  • Is there any downtime?

No. There are no incisions or needles, so you can go straight back to work. You may notice some redness, warmth, or swelling, which usually settles within a few days. Skip saunas, hot baths, intense workouts, and alcohol for a day or two, and most importantly, drink at least 2 L of water a day.

  • Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. ONDA PRO removes what's weighing you down, while Ultherapy and Thermage lift what's sagging, so the two work beautifully together. Depending on your skin, we'll do them on the same day or one to two weeks apart.

Aftercare

2 L of water a day

The single most important thing you can do. Staying well hydrated helps your body flush out the treated fat. Keep it up for at least two to four weeks.

  • Stay active with light cardio, like walking, to boost circulation.
  • Gently massage the treated area to help swelling and tenderness settle faster.
  • For warmth or redness, apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for a few minutes at a time.
  • Skip saunas, hot baths, intense workouts, and alcohol for a day or two.
  • Go easy on salty, greasy food and alcohol to help your results last.
  • Call us right away if you notice blistering, worsening pain, or numbness that doesn't go away.
