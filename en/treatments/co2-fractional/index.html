CO2 Fractional Laser

A CO2 fractional laser creates tiny, closely spaced columns in the skin, replacing damaged tissue with fresh, new skin. It's one of the most effective lasers for long-standing concerns like acne scars, enlarged pores, and fine lines.

Because healthy skin between each column drives healing, recovery is faster than with lasers that resurface the entire surface. As your skin heals, new collagen builds up, improving both texture and firmness.

Microscopic laser columns that create new skin and collagen

Acne scars · Pores · Fine lines · Texture · Scars

Video: how CO2 fractional laser works

CO2 laser energy is absorbed by the water in your skin, precisely vaporizing tiny areas of tissue. The fractional approach delivers it in a grid of dots, so damaged tissue is replaced while the surrounding healthy skin speeds recovery.

Over the following months, your skin keeps producing new collagen. That's how pitted scars fill in, enlarged pores tighten, and texture becomes smoother.

CO2 Fractional Laser: Your Questions, Answered

Who is it for?

It's a great fit for pitted acne scars, enlarged pores, surgical or injury scars, rough texture, and fine lines. We recommend it if you want a significant change and can set aside a few days for recovery.

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream thoroughly beforehand. You'll feel heat and stinging during treatment, and we cool the skin afterward to settle the warmth quickly.

What's recovery like?

Expect redness and warmth after treatment. Small crusts form after two to three days and usually fall off within five to seven days, and redness fades gradually over a few weeks. If you have an important event, leave about two weeks before it.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the depth and extent of scarring, but we usually recommend three to five sessions spaced four to eight weeks apart. New collagen takes time, so your skin keeps improving for months after your final session.

Can it cause pigmentation?

Asian skin can develop temporary pigmentation after laser treatment. We adjust the energy to your skin tone and give clear guidance on sun protection and recovery care. If pigmentation does appear, it usually fades with care.

How is it different from Secret RF?

CO2 renews the skin from the surface, making a bigger difference to deep scars and pores, but it comes with downtime. Secret RF protects the surface and delivers energy to the dermis, with almost no downtime. We sometimes alternate the two depending on your scars and schedule.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. Adding PRP right after CO2 speeds healing and boosts renewal. For deep, pitted scars, combining needle subcision and filler can deliver even better results.

Aftercare

With CO2 fractional, aftercare matters as much as the treatment itself for results and preventing pigmentation.

Sun protection

Healing skin is very sensitive to UV. Apply sunscreen diligently and avoid the sun for at least a month.

  • Don't pick at crusts; let them fall off on their own.
  • Apply the recovery cream and moisturizer we recommend generously.
  • Avoid saunas, hot baths, intense workouts, and alcohol for one week.
  • Pause exfoliants, retinol, and acid-based skincare until your skin has healed.
  • Call us right away if you notice oozing, severe pain, or lasting heat.
