What is subcision for acne scars? Subcision is a minimally invasive treatment that uses a fine needle to release the fibrous bands pulling pitted and rolling acne scars down, so the skin can lift back up. It is often combined with filler, RF microneedling or CO2 laser.

Deep, pitted acne scars form when bands of scar tissue under the skin hold it down. As long as those bands remain, even strong lasers struggle to lift the scar.

Subcision uses a fine needle to release those bands so the skin can lift back up. When needed, a small amount of filler supports the released area for a faster, smoother result.

Subcision: Your Questions, Answered

What type of acne scars does subcision work for?

Rolling scars and soft, pitted scars respond best. Narrow "ice pick" scars need other treatments.

Does subcision hurt?

The area is numbed with local anesthetic. Afterwards it may feel bruised for a few days.

How long does bruising last after subcision?

Bruising and swelling can last one to two weeks, so plan around important events.

How many subcision sessions do I need?

Usually one to three sessions, about six weeks apart.

Can acne scars be removed completely?

Scars can be improved significantly, but rarely erased. We'll tell you what's realistic for your type of scar.

Aftercare

The first week

Bruising is common, so plan important events around it.

  • Don't rub or massage the treated area.
  • Avoid saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days.
  • Apply sunscreen carefully every day.
  • Call us right away if swelling or pain gets worse.
